Install

These test builds are not signed yet, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm the first time you open the app.

  1. Run the downloaded installerOpen Tedicare-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.
  2. If “Windows protected your PC” appearsSelect More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Finish the installerChoose where to install, then open Tedicare from the desktop or Start menu shortcut.
  1. Open the downloaded disk imageChoose the file for your Mac: Apple silicon for M1 and later, Intel for older Macs. Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac shows Chip (Apple silicon) or Processor (Intel).
  2. Drag Tedicare into ApplicationsThen eject the Tedicare disk image.
  3. Open Tedicare once and allow itmacOS says it can’t verify the app. Select Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and select Open Anyway next to Tedicare, then confirm with your Mac password.
macOS 13 or 14: instead of step 3, Control-click Tedicare in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.
Which Mac? Any MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac or Mac mini with Apple silicon (M1 or later), and Intel Macs from 2017–2018 onward that can update to macOS 13 Ventura (MacBook Air: 2018 or later). Check in Apple menu → About This Mac.
